What each problem is

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so any date with a day up to 12 comes out with day and month swapped. This is the bug #4471 is named for.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type month first because the form tells them to, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. It happens only through the form, only at booking-v3 tenants. The parser fix does not touch it; the hint or the form’s read order must change.
  • shift: the date moves by one day, not a day/month swap. The same meeting shows Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto; an invite arrives before the booking. This reads as timezone handling. The desk noted these reports (desk-notes.json) but filed them under parser by tenant, so they had no ticket.

How the reports were counted

  • The export repeats some rows where its pages overlap: one id, one report.
  • Each report is placed by its tenant’s locale and booking form and its channel (#lib/kb/causes.ts), not by intake’s tag. Intake tags follow the reporter’s browser and disagree with the cause on both sides.
  • Reports the desk read as a one-day move get their own cause (#lib/kb/split.ts).
  • Every number on this page is computed from reports-4471.json at render time.

Why they ended up in one ticket

Decision 0001 merges on subject line alone. All three problems arrive under the same subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”, “Dates mixed up”), so subject matching cannot keep them apart. Split by cause, as the triage explorer already does, before marking a big ticket known.
