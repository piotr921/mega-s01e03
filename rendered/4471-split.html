What each cause is

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so any date with a day up to 12 comes out with day and month swapped. This is the bug #4471 is named for.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type month first because the form tells them to, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. It happens only through the form, only at booking-v3 tenants. The parser fix does not touch it; the hint or the form’s read order must change.

Some reports read as a one-day move, not a swap (desk-notes.json). The desk files them by tenant and the table above counts them there, with a footnote. Check them again once the parser fix ships.

How the reports are counted

  • The export repeats some rows where its pages overlap: one id, one report (uniqueById).
  • Each report is placed by its tenant’s locale and booking form and its channel (#lib/kb/causes.ts), not by intake’s tag. Intake tags follow the reporter’s browser and disagree with the cause on both sides.
  • Every number on this page comes from the desk’s own components, computed from reports-4471.json at render time. When the desk adds reports, the page follows.
