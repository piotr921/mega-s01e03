 What each cause is
 - parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day
first, so any date with a day up to 12 comes out with day and month swapped. This is the bug
#4471 is named for.
 - hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type month
first because the form tells them to, and the form reads the date the tenant's way, day first. It
happens only through the form, only at booking-v3 tenants. The parser fix does not touch it; the
hint or the form's read order must change.
 Some reports read as a one-day move, not a swap (desk-notes.json). The desk files them by tenant
and the table above counts them there, with a footnote. Check them again once the parser fix ships.
